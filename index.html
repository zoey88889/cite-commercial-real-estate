FAQ

Questions clients often ask before entering the New York commercial market.

How should a company begin a New York office space search?

Begin with use, headcount, client access, employee commute patterns, budget, lease term, and desired building identity. A disciplined search compares relevant New York submarkets before negotiating economics and flexibility.

What matters most when leasing New York warehouse space?

Loading, ceiling height, column spacing, power, zoning, truck access, parking, proximity to highways, and lease structure are central. Different New York markets serve distinct industrial and logistics needs.

What matters when evaluating New York restaurant or retail space?

Each storefront should be reviewed for venting, utilities, frontage, foot traffic, layout, licensing considerations, and the strength of the surrounding tenant mix.

Do international buyers need a different New York advisory process?

International buyers benefit from clear local market context, ownership structure considerations, income analysis, property operations planning, and a long-term real estate strategy that accounts for New York’s operating realities.
