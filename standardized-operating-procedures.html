Standard Client Operating Procedures

Pursuant to New York Real Property Law Section 442-h, CITE INTERNATIONAL REALTY CORP has adopted the following Standard Client Operating Procedures.

LAST UPDATED · August 2026

01

Applies To

Commercial real estate brokerage services in New York.

These Standard Client Operating Procedures apply to prospective clients contacting CITE INTERNATIONAL REALTY CORP regarding commercial real estate brokerage services in New York.

These procedures apply to prospective clients making an initial inquiry regarding real estate brokerage services.

03

Notice

Documentation may be requested at a later stage.

CITE INTERNATIONAL REALTY CORP does not require prospective buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants, investors, developers, or other prospective clients to provide identification, execute an exclusive brokerage agreement, or provide proof of funds as a condition of an initial inquiry, consultation, or discussion regarding real estate brokerage services.

However, depending on the specific transaction, representation, property, financing, or applicable legal requirements, additional documentation may be requested at a later stage.

Such documentation may include, but is not limited to:

  • Identification;
  • Proof of funds;
  • Brokerage representation agreements;
  • Agency disclosure forms;
  • Entity formation documents;
  • Financing documentation;
  • Property-specific documentation; or
  • Other information reasonably required to facilitate a transaction.

In addition, a property owner, seller, landlord, listing broker, lender, governmental authority, or applicable law may independently require certain documentation before permitting property access, accepting or negotiating an offer, entering into a brokerage relationship, or completing a transaction.

Nothing contained in these Standard Client Operating Procedures modifies or limits any obligations imposed by applicable federal, state, or local law, regulation, or transaction-specific requirements.
