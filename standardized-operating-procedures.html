01

Prospective Homebuyer Procedures

Standard prerequisites before receiving services.

New York requires real estate brokers to maintain and publish standardized operating procedures addressing prerequisites that prospective homebuyers may be asked to satisfy before receiving services. Our current brokerage policies are as follows:

Identification RequiredYes
Exclusive Broker Agreement RequiredYes
Proof of Funds or Mortgage Pre-Approval RequiredYes

Policy Note

CITE INTERNATIONAL REALTY CORP requires identification, an exclusive broker agreement, and proof of funds or mortgage pre-approval as standardized prerequisites before a prospective homebuyer receives services. These procedures will be applied consistently and in accordance with applicable fair housing and anti-discrimination requirements.
